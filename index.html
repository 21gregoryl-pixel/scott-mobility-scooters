Questions

Before you call

Do you deliver to my hotel?

Yes. We deliver to any hotel, resort, or vacation rental within a mile of Disneyland, and most places within about a 10-minute drive of the parks. See the map for the hotels we serve every day. That covers the whole resort district and the surrounding area: Disneyland Hotel, Pixar Place, Grand Californian, Hilton Anaheim, Westin Anaheim Resort, Sheraton Park, Four Points, Holiday Inn & Suites, Best Western Park Place, Courtyard, Days Inn, Majestic Garden, and the rest. Most of Garden Grove, Orange, and Buena Park fall inside that. Not sure? Book it and we'll confirm or refund you in full. Enter where you're staying at checkout and we'll confirm.

How far will the scooter go on one charge?

A full charge gives roughly 8 hours of typical use, enough for most park days. The battery works a little harder for heavier riders, on hills, and at top speed, so range runs shorter in those cases and longer in the opposite ones. Plan on a bit less than 8 hours if you're near the 360 lb capacity or spend the day at full throttle. Plug it in overnight in your room using the charger we provide and it's ready again by morning.

Is there a weight limit?

Yes, 360 lb. That covers the large majority of riders comfortably. If you're close to it, the scooter still works fine; you'll just see a shorter battery range, so charge fully each night and take the flat routes when you can.

Do I need a license or a medical note?

No license or prescription is needed to rent a mobility scooter. Riders must be adults and sign our rental agreement and waiver at delivery.

What if the scooter stops working?

Text (714) 917-7763 and we bring a replacement to you, at your hotel or at the gates of the theme park, whichever is closer to where you are. No charge, no paperwork, you keep your day.

Can I change or cancel my dates?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or text the number on it and we'll move your dates. See the rental agreement below for the cancellation window.

How do I book and pay?

Tap any Book now button. Booking is run by Square: pick your delivery date, enter your hotel and card, and you get a confirmation email instantly. $75 per day, paid at booking, no deposit. If a date doesn't show up, every scooter is already reserved that day.

I need more than one day or more than one scooter.

Each booking reserves one scooter for one day. For more days, book each date you need. For more scooters, book the same date again, once per scooter. Each one is held under your name.
