Why rent before you go

Getting a scooter at the park is the hardest part of the day.

Park rental counters open with the gates, hand out scooters first come first served, and are usually empty by mid-morning. That means lining up early, hoping one is left, and walking back to the counter to return it before closing.

We skip all of that. Your scooter is at your Anaheim hotel the night before, charged and reserved under your name. Ride it from the lobby, through the gates, around the park, and back to your room. Park days are long; you should spend yours enjoying them, not chasing a rental.

Questions

Before you call

Do you deliver to my hotel?

Yes. We deliver to hotels, resorts, and vacation rentals throughout Anaheim and the resort district. Enter where you're staying at checkout.

How far will the scooter go on one charge?

We guarantee 8 hours of use on a full charge, enough for a full park day. Plug it in overnight in your room using the charger we provide and it's ready again by morning.

Do I need a license or a medical note?

No license or prescription is needed to rent a mobility scooter. Riders must be adults and sign our rental agreement and waiver at delivery.

What if the scooter stops working?

Text the number on the scooter. We bring a replacement to your hotel. Swaps are free.

Can I change or cancel my dates?

Yes. Reply to your confirmation email or text the number on it and we'll move your dates. See the rental agreement below for the cancellation window.

How do I book and pay?

Tap any Book now button. Checkout is run by Square: enter your hotel, dates, and card, and you get a confirmation email instantly. $75 per day, no deposit.

I need more than one day or more than one scooter.

Checkout charges $75 per booking. For extra days or extra scooters, run the checkout once per scooter-day, or book one and we'll settle the rest by card at delivery.
